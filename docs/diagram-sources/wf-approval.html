Design decisions

1
Actions only when they make sense (Core.OperationAvailable): Approve / Reject for Submitted, Update in S/4HANA for Approved. Reject needs a reason.
2
Header KPIs answer “what happens if I approve?” – status before and after, value change, method.
3
Current, suggested and proposed values in three columns of one section.
4
Basis and effect: demand from S/4HANA goods issues and the expected stock situation.
5
The exact S/4HANA payload is shown (transparency; simulated in the read-only sandbox).
6
Audit trail: every step with user, time and note.
i
Fiori elements gives the standard list/object pattern, filters, variants and export with annotations instead of UI code.
