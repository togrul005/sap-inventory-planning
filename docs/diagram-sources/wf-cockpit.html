Design decisions

1
One primary action (Sync from S/4HANA, emphasized). The connection check shows each API and the update mode.
2
Management by exception: KPI tiles count the materials per situation; a click filters the table.
3
Semantic colours (red Critical, orange Reorder, green Healthy) plus icons, so the status is not shown by colour alone.
4
Current vs suggested side by side, with the deviation in % – the reason why a planner should act.
5
Propose is emphasized only when the deviation is 25 % or more, and disabled while a change is open.
6
Coverage in days is red when the stock runs out before a delivery could arrive (coverage < lead time).
7
Tabs keep the Changes (with Withdraw) and the Sync Log one click away; the open change is linked in the row.
i
Responsive: less important columns move into a pop-in on tablets and phones.
