Design decisions

1
Facts first: demand, variation, number of goods issues, period window and coverage, all from SAP S/4HANA.
2
Explainable suggestion: the formula with the material's own numbers, so planner and approver can follow it (no black box).
3
One click takes over the suggestion; the MRP type line shows a switch to VB if needed.
4
Three columns Current – Suggested – New: the decision happens in one place.
5
Changing the lead time recalculates the suggestion on the server (safety stock grows with √lead time).
6
Live preview of the stock status and the value tied up in safety stock; inline validation with value states.
7
A reason is mandatory – it becomes part of the audit trail the approver reads.
